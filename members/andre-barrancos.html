André Barrancos was born in Lisbon, Portugal, in 1997. He received the B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Electronics Engineering from Instituto Superior Técnico (IST) in 2020 and 2021, respectively. He’s currently a researcher at Instituto de Telecomunicações (IT). His research focuses on microfluidics, electromagnetic sensors for microplastics detection, and the development of novel electromagnetic techniques for layer-wise inspection in powder bed fusion metal additive manufacturing. In parallel, he’s pursuing a PhD at IST, focused on the development of a smart sensing system for real-time microplastics’ detection. This project has been recognized for its impact and innovation, being selected as one of the winners of the “Programa Transformar” and awarded a 10,000 € grant. Also, he’s an invited teaching assistant for the Department of Computer Engineering (DEI) at IST. His catchphrase is “Oh xerife!”

Project Title: Smart Sensing System for Real-Time Microplastics Detection Supervisors: Luís Rosado, Pedro Ramos and João Felício
