Victor S. Macedo is a researcher in Electronics Engineering with background in capacitive sensors, embedded measurement systems, mixed-signal and digital signal processing, as well as semiconductor design. In 2025, he completed a double-degree program in Electronics Engineering through a collaboration between the University of São Paulo (USP) and Instituto Superior Técnico (IST). His MSc. dissertation, focused on developing the first conceptual capacitive sensor array probe designed for layer-by-layer imaging in metal 3D printing processes. After a year in industry, Victor returned to academia and was accepted in the ‘DEEC call for PhD applications’, a temporary PhD scholarship program designed to support the transition to other funding sources. The program supports students of high academic merit who are interested in pursuing a PhD at Instituto Superior Técnico, funded through a part-time teaching position. His primary research interests include capacitive sensors, eddy current sensors, electronics measurement systems and signal processing applied to the quality control of additive manufacturing processes.

Project Title: Novel capacitive sensor arrays and advanced digital signal processing for layer-by-layer powder bed fusion quality control Supervisors: Luís Rosado and Pedro Ramos
