Tiago Fernandes investigated a near-field microwave probe for the rapid, non-destructive detection of microplastics in samples, with the aim of enabling on-site analysis as an alternative to time-consuming laboratory techniques. He designed and experimentally evaluated a probe operating at 3.35 GHz, comparing its performance with simulation results that predicted an approximately 100 µW signal difference between samples with and without microplastics. Although the experimental sensitivity was lower than expected, the results demonstrated the feasibility of microwave-based microplastic detection and identified opportunities to improve the probe’s sensitivity and characterization capabilities.
