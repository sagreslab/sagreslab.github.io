André was born in Leiria, Portugal, in 2003. He received the B.Sc. degree in Electronics Engineering from IST in 2025, and he is currently pursuing the M.Sc. degree in Electronics Engineering at IST. He is developing his master’s thesis at ITel, under the supervision of Prof. Luís S. Rosado, focused on a high-throughput real-time microscopic imaging system based on 1D CMOS line sensors for microfluidics-assisted microplastics assessment. His research interests include microfluidics, image sensors, signal acquisition and embedded systems. In parallel, he works as a Field Systems & Electronics Engineer at JJL, Lda., providing technical support for hospital logistics and communication systems. During his B.Sc., he took part in the Electrocap capstone project, developing a drone-based thermal imaging system for remote forest fire detection. His catchphrase is “Bem tenso!”.
