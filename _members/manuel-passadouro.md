Manuel Passadouro developed a prototype micro-scale Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (µ-WAAM) system by adapting a low-cost FDM 3D printer for metal deposition. His work combined mechanical design, power electronics, embedded control, and software integration, including a high-frequency power converter, real-time voltage and current monitoring, and a graphical interface synchronized with the printer's motion system. Experimental tests demonstrated stable voltage regulation and reliable overcurrent protection, while also identifying key challenges in arc initiation, wire feeding, and process stability. This work establishes a foundation for further development of accessible, low-cost platforms for research into metal additive manufacturing at sub-millimeter scales.
