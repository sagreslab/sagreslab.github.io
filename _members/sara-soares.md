Sara Soares developed a flow control system for a portable platform aimed at the rapid detection of airborne pathogenic agents using microfluidics and magnetoresistive cytometry. Her work focused on designing a flow rate sensor based on heat dispersion to monitor fluid velocity within microfluidic channels, enabling stable and controlled sample delivery. She also implemented PWM-based micropump control and developed a graphical interface for wireless operation of the pump and selected air sampler functions. This work contributes to the integration of flow sensing, embedded electronics, and wireless control in portable platforms for rapid, on-site pathogen detection.
