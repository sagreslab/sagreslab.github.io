Bernardo Sebastião developed a compact, low-cost platform for the electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) of lithium-ion batteries as part of his MSc thesis. His work combined embedded electronics and digital signal processing to build a system capable of measuring battery impedance over a frequency range of 0.05 Hz to 1 kHz, while also performing controlled charge–discharge cycles to monitor capacity and ageing. By integrating multisine excitation, electrical response acquisition, and impedance analysis with equivalent circuit modelling, Bernardo demonstrated the potential of combining EIS and battery cycling in a single platform for battery condition monitoring and state-of-health assessment.
