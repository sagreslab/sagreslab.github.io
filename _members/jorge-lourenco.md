Jorge Lourenço developed and validated an embedded system for characterizing lithium-ion batteries using electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS). His work combined multiharmonic excitation with efficient embedded signal processing based on the Goertzel algorithm to measure battery impedance across a frequency range of 50 mHz to 1 kHz, while minimizing memory requirements. By characterizing a battery under different states of charge and health, Jorge developed an equivalent electrical model and investigated how its parameters vary with battery condition. This work provides a foundation for future embedded solutions capable of estimating battery state of charge and state of health through impedance measurements.
