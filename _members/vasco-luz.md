Vasco Luz developed an application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) for scalable eddy current testing (ECT) systems, addressing the need for compact, low-noise electronics capable of processing signals from multiple inductive sensors. The ASIC integrates a low-noise amplifier with 20 dB gain at 10 MHz, a programmable-gain amplifier with four gain settings, a fully differential quadrature demodulator for extracting in-phase and quadrature signal components, and a subtracting amplifier for reference-based signal processing. Designed in TSMC 65 nm low-power technology, the prototype was submitted for fabrication through the Europractice-supported micro-ASIC project. This work contributes to the development of compact, integrated readout electronics for next-generation ECT systems, including applications in metal additive manufacturing inspection.
