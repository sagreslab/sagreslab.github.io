Markiyan Pyekh developed a modular instrument for semi-automatic eddy current testing, combining electromagnetic scanning, control software, and AI-based data reconstruction. His work included the implementation of a planar dual-driver probe and software for configuring, testing, and operating the measurement system. He also developed a deep learning approach based on a convolutional neural network to reconstruct and visualize scanned surfaces from eddy current measurements, using artificially generated defect patterns for training. The results demonstrated promising noise resistance and generalization, contributing to the development of intelligent electromagnetic inspection systems for non-destructive testing.
