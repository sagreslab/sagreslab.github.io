Mariana Teixeira developed a modular transimpedance amplifier front-end for optical interferometry-based measurement of MEMS in-plane displacement. Her work combined analog electronics design, component selection, and custom PCB development to enable the acquisition of high-frequency optical signals up to 10 MHz. The system was characterized in the laboratory and subsequently integrated with photodiodes in a Mach–Zehnder interferometer setup at the International Iberian Nanotechnology Laboratory (INL). By processing the acquired interferometric signals, Mariana demonstrated the system's ability to measure sub-micrometer displacements, establishing a foundation for pursuing sub-nanometer resolution in advanced inertial sensing, gravimetry, and precision measurement applications.
