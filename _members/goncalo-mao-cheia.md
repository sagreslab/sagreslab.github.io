Gonçalo Mão-Cheia continued the development of a battery-powered, connected eddy current testing system for structural health monitoring. His work integrated electromagnetic sensing with temperature and acceleration measurements, alongside a graphical user interface for configuring the device and visualizing acquired data via Bluetooth. The system also supports autonomous periodic measurements and remote monitoring through LTE connectivity and the ThingSpeak cloud platform. Experimental validation demonstrated its ability to detect a large crack in an aluminium plate, highlighting the potential of combining electromagnetic sensing and IoT technologies for the continuous monitoring of metallic structures and the development of smarter, more accessible inspection solutions.
