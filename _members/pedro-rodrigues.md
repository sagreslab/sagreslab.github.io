Pedro Rodrigues developed a coplanar capacitive sensor integrated with a microfluidic channel for detecting individual microplastic particles in water. Using finite-element simulations and an experimental prototype with an AD7746 capacitance-to-digital converter, he demonstrated the detection of single microplastic beads, measuring capacitance variations of approximately 0.1 fF. The work validates the potential of capacitive sensing for microplastic detection and identifies signal amplitude and measurement noise as key challenges for further development.
