Rodrigo Caldeira was born in Lisbon, Portugal, in 2004. In 2025 he earned a B.Sc. degree in Electronics Engineering and is currently engaged in a M.Sc. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering, both at Instituto Superior Técnico (IST). Upon beginning his master's, Rodrigo joined SagresLab, a research group from Instituto de Telecomunicações (IT), where he is currently carrying out the development of his thesis. His work focuses on Battery Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy, searching a modular and scalable approach which enables the improvement of battery technology in an increasingly electrified world.
He is known to spend his days in the workshop a couple floors bellow the main Sagres' offices, which earned him the nickname "Homem da cave" (Basement man).
