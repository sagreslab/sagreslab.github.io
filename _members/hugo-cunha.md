Hugo Cunha investigated vibration energy harvesting as a potential solution for powering small-scale electronic devices and wireless sensors autonomously. His work focused on electromagnetic energy conversion using a magnetic levitation transducer, which he designed and built, exploring its potential to convert mechanical vibrations into usable electrical energy. To evaluate its performance, Hugo developed an experimental characterization setup capable of testing the transducer under both sinusoidal excitation and more complex vibration profiles simulating real-world sources. This work contributes to the development and characterization of energy harvesting technologies for self-powered sensing systems and IoT applications.
