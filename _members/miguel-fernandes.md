Miguel Fernandes developed and validated an electronic control system to stabilize the electric arc in micro-scale Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (µ-WAAM). His work combined analog and digital electronics, integrating a custom control circuit with a digital processor implementing signal filtering, a PI controller, and a dynamic adjustment algorithm to regulate arc current and voltage. The controller was tested within a real µ-WAAM pilot installation, demonstrating the feasibility of active electric-arc stabilization for metal 3D printing. This work contributes to improving process stability and provides a foundation for more reliable and controlled metal additive manufacturing at the micro-scale.
