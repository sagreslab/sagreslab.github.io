Pedro Coutinho developed a low-cost coulomb integrator for estimating the state of charge (SoC) of batteries powering wireless IoT devices. His work explored an alternative to conventional ADC-based coulomb counting, using an RC integration circuit, transistor-based capacitor reset, and an enhanced comparator to measure accumulated charge through pulse counting. This work demonstrates the potential of a simple, low-cost and energy-efficient electronic architecture for battery monitoring in autonomous IoT devices.
