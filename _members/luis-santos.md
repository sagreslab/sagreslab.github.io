Luis Artur developed a photovoltaic fault-emulation module based on a 5×5 array of small solar panels and a microcontroller-controlled switching network, enabling the generation of characteristic curves under different fault conditions. He implemented and validated a threshold-based diagnostic algorithm that identified fault types with 89% accuracy on experimentally measured curves. The work demonstrates a low-cost platform for generating and analysing photovoltaic faults, supporting the development of simpler diagnostic solutions for small and medium-sized installations.
