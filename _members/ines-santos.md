Inês Santos, born in 2003, received her B.Sc. degree in Electronics Engineering from Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), University of Lisbon, Portugal, in 2025. She is currently pursuing two M.Sc. degrees at IST: Electrical and Computer Engineering, specialising in Electronic Circuits and Systems, and Aerospace Engineering, specialising in Avionics. Her research, conducted as part of her M.Sc. thesis in Electrical and Computer Engineering, focuses on electronic instrumentation and impedance spectroscopy applied to photovoltaic panel characterisation and condition assessment. Alongside her studies, she joined JEEC’s Marketing team in 2023 and became its Team Leader in 2025. She also served as a class representative and student mentor at IST, and as a group monitor for the Verão na ULisboa summer programme.
