João Santos, born in Lisbon, Portugal, in 2004, received his B. Sc. Degree in Aerospace Engineering in 2025, from IST, during which he was awarded three Academic Merit Diplomas. Alongside this degree, he integrated the ATLAS – Systems and Aeronautics project, performing various roles, such as team leader and avionics coordinator. This experience led him to pursue a more specialized path in the field of electronics, enrolling on the Electrical and Computer Engineering master’s degree at IST with a major in electronic circuits and systems. He is currently a second year double degree student, integrating both IST and UPC (Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya) and staying at the latter. His research interests include low-power integrated circuit design, oscillator-based capacitive microplastic detection systems and integrated circuit testing and characterization. As a hobby, he enjoys building and flying multirotor drones.
