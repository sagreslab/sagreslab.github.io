Jorge Assis, born in April 2003, received the B.Sc. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from IST in 2024, where he is currently pursuing the M.Sc. degree. During his studies, he was awarded two Academic Merit Diploma and an Academic Excellence Diploma in recognition of his academic performance. His research interests focus on electromagnetic sensing, particularly the development of high-resolution eddy-current sensor arrays and resonant multiplexed readout architectures for structural health monitoring applications. As part of this research effort, he authored a conference publication and co-authored a published scientific article. He is currently a teaching assistant, serving courses offered by the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (DEEC) of IST.
