Jorge Assis, born in April 2003, received the B.Sc. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), University of Lisbon, Portugal, in 2024, where he is currently pursuing the M.Sc. degree. During his studies, he was awarded two Academic Merit Diploma and an Academic Excellence Diploma in recognition of his academic performance. His research interests focus on electromagnetic sensing, particularly the development of high-resolution eddy-current sensor arrays and resonant multiplexed readout architectures for structural health monitoring applications. As part of this research effort, he authored a conference publication and co-authored a published scientific article. He is currently a Teaching Assistant (TA) for the curricular units Big-Data Measuring Systems and Instrumentation and Measurements at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (DEEC) of IST, and previously served as a TA for Circuit Analysis and Instrumentation and Measurements in 2024/25.
