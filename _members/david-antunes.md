Project Title: Electromagnetic Structures for High Spatial Resolution Eddy Current Testing
David Antunes investigated how excitation coil geometries can improve the spatial resolution of eddy current testing for quality monitoring in metal additive manufacturing. Using CST Studio Suite, he simulated and compared different configurations to optimize the confinement of induced currents and the detection of small defects during layer-by-layer inspection of laser powder bed fusion components. His work led to the design of an improved multi-coil eddy current probe capable of detecting simulated stainless steel features as small as 0.25 mm in radius, while maintaining the existing scanning speed and operating frequency. This work contributes to the development of high-resolution electromagnetic sensing solutions for real-time quality control in metal 3D printing.
