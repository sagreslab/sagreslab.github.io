Diogo Rosinha developed a near-field microwave microscopy (NFMM) platform for non-destructive material imaging and analysis. His work combined the design of frequency-tuned probes, microwave generation and measurement hardware, and software for visualizing acquired data, resulting in an integrated and scalable system that can accommodate multiple probes. The developed hardware operates from 35 MHz to 4.4 GHz, with an output power of 5 dBm, and achieved performance comparable to a professional real-time spectrum analyser at almost 100 times lower cost. This work lays the foundations for accessible, flexible microwave sensing solutions for material characterization and non-destructive testing.
