Pedro Faria developed an advanced 240-coil eddy current probe array for layer-by-layer quality control of metal parts produced by Powder Bed Fusion (PBF). Integrated into the printer's recoater, the system enables in-situ inspection and imaging during fabrication, achieving spatial resolutions down to 0.16 mm at scanning speeds of 250 mm/s. This work demonstrates the potential of high-density electromagnetic sensor arrays for real-time monitoring of metal additive manufacturing processes.
